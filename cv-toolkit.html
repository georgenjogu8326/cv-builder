Build a clean CV in minutes. Free.

Fill in your details, pick a layout, and save it as a PDF. Nothing is uploaded. Your CV stays on your device.

Layout
About you Two or three sentences: what you do, what you are good at, what you want next.
Experience Write a heading line for each job, then bullet lines starting with a dash (-).
Education
Skills Separate skills with commas.
In the print window, choose “Save as PDF” as the destination.
The job
Your pitch Example: In my current role I handle 60+ customer queries a day and keep satisfaction above 95%. Finish the sentence “I am drawn to the company because…” Example: it helps small businesses get online.
Your name and contact details come from the CV tab.

Tip: keep your CV to one page, use a professional email address, and tailor the summary to each job you apply for.
